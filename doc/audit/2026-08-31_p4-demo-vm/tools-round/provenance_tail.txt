--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What was verified on this image, and what was not
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Verified, on the packaged artefact itself, not on a working copy:

  * each of the four trees builds from source here (the pre-built binaries that came
    with two of the clones were deleted first, so what you have was compiled here)
  * each program starts: energy_saving_app serves :8001, simulation_platform_manager
    serves :9000, request_manager serves :8002, the Visualizer opens its window under
    a display, Traffic-engineering-App.py reaches its menu, and
    energy_saving_simulator runs to completion on an input file
  * a cold boot of this image listens on nothing but sshd and the local DNS stub
  * the P4/BMv2 fabric still comes up and passes traffic: 10 switches, 12 paths,
    40 edges, pingall 0% dropped (12/12)

NOT verified, and stated because the difference matters:

  * that these applications talk to THIS kernel's API. The four trees are from
    2026-04-20; the kernel in this image is from 2026-08-28. "Builds and starts" is
    not "interoperates", and the second was not tested.
