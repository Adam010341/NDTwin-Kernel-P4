--- dfdb2121 tests/shell: count a fixture that never takes its argv as a failed check
Six suites started their fixtures with X="$(spawn_fixture ...)", so the
helper's exit 1 on a failed exec only left the command substitution: the
suite ran on with the helper's text in place of a pid and never counted the
failure, and test_ndtwin_lab_sweep.sh, under the errexit its sourced
ndtwin-lab leaves on, stopped without any summary line.

The helpers are now plain calls that leave the pid in FIXTURE_PID. The
premise is a counted check, a failure prints the summary and exits 1, and
the argv poll waits up to 30 s instead of 1-2 s. The new
mutate_fixture_spawn_helpers.sh makes each helper never match and requires
the failure to be counted.
