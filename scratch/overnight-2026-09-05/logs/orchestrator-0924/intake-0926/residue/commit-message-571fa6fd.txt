ndt: date apps and rules against one clock read per residue report

residue_report read `now` once and app_started_at read the clock again for a
live app, so a second boundary between the two dated the app a second late:
a rule an app had just installed, or one from its first second, fell outside
its window. This is the intermittent test_apps_residue failure on CI.

The report now reads `now` once, after the flow table, and dates every app
right after it with that same value, before the lock probes, so a slow
acquire cannot open a window early either. The stop-path capture and the
windows whose edges are recorded instants are not covered by this and are
recorded as G-62.

test_apps_residue keeps a young fixture in 5K and waits for its argv instead
of carrying on, and gains 5N, which forces a boundary between the reads, slow
lock probes and a fetch that straddles a second. A mutation gate covers both
the product and the test's own premises; the gates and ndt_serve citations
that quote the moved lines are updated.
