The proxy now builds ternary, range and optional entries and pre-flight
checks them with the writer's own shape rules instead of refusing them, so
PF-T has to pass. S0 now requires pre-flight to accept the PF-T package and

entry is recognised by where pre-flight names it, not by the old sentence.

the predictions for T4-T7 and PF-T move to GREEN. The PF-T fixture is
recaptured from a real pre-flight run, with a captured refusal beside it.
