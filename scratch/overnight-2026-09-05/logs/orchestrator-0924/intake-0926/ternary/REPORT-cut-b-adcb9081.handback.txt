3. The op to journal for a default insert is the wire op (modify), since the writer substitutes. Design silent; the fold's default rule works either way.

5. 5.4 step 6 "snapshot = header + net records": a net op is not a record. I added records_from_net and the property fold(snapshot) == net, which is tested.

7. fold and write_snapshot have no production caller until Cut C (the repo's most-repeated defect); unit-tested only. Flagged on purpose.
8. 5.3 holds a threading.Lock across the gRPC write. A hung bmv2 ties up one threadpool thread per queued POST to that switch (up to RPC_TIMEOUT_S each). The design says "milliseconds"; no cap or fail-fast.
